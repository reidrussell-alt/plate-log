### Changed
- **New welcome screen (16 Nov 2026).** Opening the app now shows the plate logo on its own, then a calm, slowly turning ring of dots shaped like a weight plate, with "Welcome in, Reid" and one green "Let's get moving" button that takes you to Home. Less to read, no workout pushed at you, and it waits for you rather than vanishing on a timer. If your phone is set to reduce motion, the dots stay still.
