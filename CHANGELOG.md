# Lift Tracker - Changelog

All notable changes to the Lift Tracker application.
Written for non-technical stakeholders - focuses on user value, not implementation.

---

## [Unreleased]

### Changed
- **Warmer, calmer colours (16 Nov 2026).** The app now has a white background, soft warm-grey cards and one burnt-orange colour, used only where it matters: the main button on each screen, your progress during a workout (the progress bar, ticked sets, finished exercises) and the logo. The logo and home-screen icon change from red to the same orange so everything feels like one product. Tabs, filters and switches show what's selected in dark grey rather than colour. Delete and discard buttons are now red, so they never look like the main action. Text and buttons were checked for readability.
- **A warm glow on Home (16 Nov 2026).** The top of the Home screen now has a soft burnt-orange colour that fades to white as you scroll down, with the date in black and a white Start button. The other screens stay plain white.
- **Clearer workout colours (16 Nov 2026).** Push days and the Chest chart are now plum and the Core chart teal, so nothing else looks like the orange used for buttons and progress. Remove and delete buttons and error messages are consistently red.
- **New welcome screen (16 Nov 2026).** Opening the app now shows the plate logo on its own, then a calm, slowly turning ring of dots shaped like a weight plate, with "Welcome in, Reid" and one green "Let's get moving" button that takes you to Home. Less to read, no workout pushed at you, and it waits for you rather than vanishing on a timer. If your phone is set to reduce motion, the dots stay still.
