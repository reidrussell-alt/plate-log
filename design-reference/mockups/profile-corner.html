Your photo and name in the top corner

Pattern 5 — only the round photo in the top right of the PayPal home screen is taken as inspiration. Shown with the initial "R" because no photo is set; your photo fills the circle once you add one.

How the options differ

  • A · Pill — name and a small photo in one rounded chip, on its own row above the page title. Quiet; reads as one button.
  • B · Round photo — closest to the reference: a large round photo with a white ring and your name beside it in plain text, on its own row.
  • C · On the title line — no extra row; the photo and name share the line with the date or page title, so nothing moves down. On Home the streak drops under the weekday; on My Plans the + moves next to the title.

Same in every option

  • Tapping it opens the Profile tab. Shown on Home, My Plans, Progress and Profile; not on the workout screen, which keeps its own Back and reorder buttons.
  • On Profile it sits over the cover, and the change-background button moves to the left. It could instead be left off Profile, since the big photo is already there.
  • Colours are the existing tokens; nothing orange is added.
